FACEBOOK INTERACTIVE FUNNEL ANALYSIS FOR SOCIAL NETWORKING  
As a summer intern at Facebook, I worked within Growth Infrastructure in data engineering on quick promotions on News Feed. Quick promotions are suggestions on the top of News Feed encouraging quick actions of engagement from users. Some examples include changing your profile picture, uploading contact info, and sharing videos of memories with friends. Teams within Facebook that made these promotions wanted to see how well specific promotions were performing, and if they were not, where they were not performing. Because this project involved a service that multiple teams would be using, the solution was to create an internal data pipeline and dashboard for funnel analysis. My work involed finding relevant tables for event logs, the entire process of ETL, digesting event data in a format that could be read easily, and modifying an internal tool for funnel analysis of this information. As such, I worked across the entire software stack, from data pipelining in Python, to extraction in SQL, to data visualization in React and Hack. This tool would show the stages of funnel analysis: users exposed to the quick promotion, users that viewed, users that clicked, and users that converted to executing an action (e.g. profile picture change).
