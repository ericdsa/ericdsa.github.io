SPOTIFY DJ MUSIC TRACK SORTING  
While planning music for a dance party a few summers ago, I hit a roadblock - I had no idea how quickly music would speed up or slow down, nor what music should play relative to each moment in the party. At CalHacks 3.0, I hacked together an initial data visualization prototype to address this. Users can import their playlists and see various visualizations of their playlist. Tools: Pen + Paper, Adobe XD
IDEATION We considered many ideas, and the main struggle was on how much control to give to the user vs operations done by a machine or algorithm.
CONCEPT SKETCHES We eventually finalized on emphasizing visualizations of existing playlists, the ability to control from afar when the songs should crossfade, and an algorithm to automatically sort songs in the optimal order for a party. We sketched what the mobile and web controls would look like and made a user flow. divided in to main screens: see playlist, add song, organize.
WIREFRAMES We made initial wireframes to explore placement of content, new user experience, and user interactions. Focus on seeing playlist, add song, organize.
FINAL PROTOTYPE We then went to a final prototype, which you can try out below! We also added a mobile component for controlling the playlist from afar. Focus on onboarding, button interactions, controlling after you set an order.
