ELECTRIC GYM BIKE SUSTAINABLE ELECTRICITY GENERATION  
Our final project in DES INV 21 (Visual Communication) was to design and present a product that addressed climate change. For our product, we chose to design a gym bike that charges a portable battery as a person bikes on it. Collaborators: Kathleen Hsu, Alexis Leoni
Tools: Pen + Paper, Garage Band, iMovie, Keynote
IDEATION For ideation, we were very general in concepts, since the prompt was to address climate change in whatever way possible; we explored concepts from community forest planting to more efficient systems for monitoring home energy use. We decided on an electricity-generating gym bike because we wanted to develop a product that could be used in the every day person's daily routine, and exercise was a good point to start on.
ONE-PAGER We developed a one pager with a sketch of our product and its main attributes. In particular, we chose a unicycle for a more fashionable, trendy design.
DISPLAY DESIGN Then, we went to designing a display that would sit on the gym bike like any other gym bike. We placed careful thought on what statistics were on the display, and emphasized more statistics on environmental impact, and less on typical calories burned and other workout numbers.
STORYBOARDING To showcase our product, we developed a storyboard and script for a corresponding video.
