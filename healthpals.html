HEALTHPALS DATA VISUALIZATIONS IN HEALTHCARE TECHNOLOGY  
HealthPals is a high growth startup that I met at the UC Berkeley Startup Fair, and later joined as an intern. Their mission is to create healthcare data science tools for chronic diseases. My work involved planning data schema for new incoming data and developing data visualizations of patient data. This led to full stack work: database schema and querying (Redis), server-side work (Python Pyramid), front end frameworks (AngularJS), and data visualization (Javascript). Team: The company (6 person startup!)
Tools: Python, Javascript, AngularJS
