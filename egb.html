PORTFOLIO RESUME ABOUT ERIC D'SA
ELECTRIC GYM BIKE SUSTAINABLE ELECTRICITY GENERATION  
Our final project in DES INV 21 (Visual Communication) was to design and present a product that addressed climate change. For our product, we chose to design a gym bike that charges a portable battery as a person bikes on it. Collaborators: Kathleen Hsu, Alexis Leoni
Tools: Pen + Paper, Garage Band, iMovie, Keynote IDEATION For ideation, we were very general in concepts, since the prompt was to address climate change in whatever way possible; we explored concepts from community forest planting to more efficient systems for monitoring home energy use. We wanted to develop a product that could be used in the average person's daily routine, and focused on products that are integrated into our everyday lives. Exercise is a common habit that many people are familiar with, and so we decided on an electricity-generating gym bike. ONE-PAGER We developed a one pager as a first shot at defining our product and its main characteristics. We opted for a unicycle instead of a bicycle for a more fashionable design. An LCD panel on the handlebars would display statistics in a manner similar to other gym bikes. Near the bottom of the back wheel, we placed the power generator and a pack to place batteries in. DISPLAY DESIGN We went to designing a display that would sit on the gym bike. Since our gym bike was made to address climate change, we placed careful thought on what statistics were on the display to emphasize the sustainability impact. Along these lines, our workout statistics were on trees saved and electricity generated, as opposed to more typical workout numbers like intensity intervals. STORYBOARDING We created a video to showcase our product. We first determined a storyline we wanted to show. We started with how people want to help the environment but struggle with everyday actions to help. Then, we present our product as a solution and show how its fits into our daily routine, with all the benefits of electric power. Once we finished our story arc, we wrote a script with each section. Following this, we drew sketches as frames that we animated for a video. VIDEO Check out our video!
