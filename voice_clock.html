VOICE ALARM CLOCK A DIFFERENT WAY OF WAKING UP  
Intro to how the idea came up Notes Intro People hate getting up, and the first reaction to hearing a blaring alarm clock is to shut it off. And this is how we start our day. Our very first moment we experience in the day is that of irritation, and it shouldn't be that way. I explore other ways that we might wake up, both to better wake us up, and to start our day on a better note. Tools: Pen + Paper, Voice, Adobe Illustrator IDEATION Description - GIF of waking up someone Description - need a different clock, different ideas Ideation We present a conversation alarm clock, which talks to you to get you excited to get out of bed and start your day. Initially, you would tell a topic for the alarm clock to remember. Alarm clock would pull information overnight and select the best information to present. Alternate - variety alarm clock - smartphone app THOUGHTS/REFLECTIONS FROM IDEATION Description - analogy of voice to someone else waking you up Thoughts/Reflection from Ideation The alarm rings, we wake up, lazily snooze it, and go back to sleep. This happens every day, we become habituated to it. However, when different events happens from our routine, we excitedly wake up, because of the novelty. Our alarms should not be repetitive either. Most of the time, we hate waking up. At that moment in time, all we want to do is snooze and fall back into bed, leaving consequences until later. There are two main pain points: 1) Habituation - we get used to the alarm sound, and don't react as much to it 2) Irritation - The blaring sound of an alarm annoys us, and the quickest way to comfort is to snooze and go back to bed. The blaring sound is the way we start our day; in other words, we start irritated. Why should we start our day like this? In each of these cases, the incentive is only to shut the alarm off, not get out of bed and start the day. However, when we have one-off events/celebrations, we're excited to get out of bed, often earlier than needed. Many alarm clocks lack daily novelty and a reason to start the day. They seek to annoy you to remove the problem, and hopefully wake up by the time the problem is addressed. habituation -> daily change irritation -> excitement We address this problem by changing the challenges daily for novelty. We can also create situations that excite users in the morning. Conversational, planner, daily challenges EVALUATION OF IDEATION IDEAS Description - thinking about general flow, inspiration from parent waking you up Evaluation of Ideation Ideas 1) Conversational - talk about exciting events in your day 2) Planner - schedule an exciting event in the morning, then remind you of it 3) Daily Challenges - smartphone app that changes the challenge you do every day. You don't know the challenge until the alarm rings in the morning. (2) is hard to schedule every day. (3) possible, but need to keep creating new activities. (1) depends on calendar, most tailored to you.
